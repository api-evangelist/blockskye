Enterprise travel teams gain real-time spend visibility and compliance control with AI, while travelers earn loyalty points and status on every trip. Boston, MA, January 28, 2026: Blockskye today announced the launch of AI-powered Blockskye Capture™, a new expense tracking and reimbursement solution for enterprise travel programs. Capture allows employees to use their preferred personal credit cards for business travel expenses while maximizing loyalty points and status, while giving corporate buyers real-time visibility, policy controls, and audit-ready records.

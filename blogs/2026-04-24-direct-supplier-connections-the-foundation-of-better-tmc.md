Travel managers are often asked to deliver three things at once: lower costs, better traveler experiences, and stronger supplier relationships. But the structure of corporate travel distribution doesn’t always make that easy. Most enterprise travel programs still operate on infrastructure shaped decades ago, long before modern APIs, real-time data, or direct connectivity were even possible.
